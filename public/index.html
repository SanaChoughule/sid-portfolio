02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

NOT JUST A
CAMERA GUY.

I'm Siddharth — an automobile engineer, motorcycle enthusiast and digital creator from Mumbai. I've spent years learning how motorcycles work, from vintage machines to modern bikes, and more than two years turning that knowledge into content.

My edge is knowing the subject before I press record. I understand the problems riders face, the details worth showing and the moments that make a story feel real. I create videos for my own platforms and for a riding gear and accessories store, while exploring food, lifestyle and other formats too.
