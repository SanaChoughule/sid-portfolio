A LITTLE ABOUT ME

MORE THAN
JUST CAMERAS.

I'm Siddharth — an automobile engineer, motorcycle enthusiast and hands-on content creator from Mumbai.

I've spent years around motorcycles, understanding how they work, how to maintain them and what makes each machine different — from vintage bikes to modern motorcycles. That technical foundation shapes the way I create.

I have been creating content for over two years, including videos for a riding gear and accessories store. I enjoy finding the everyday rider problem, building a strong hook around it, and turning the solution into a story that feels useful and relatable.

I also explore food, lifestyle and other formats, collaborating on content beyond the automotive space.
